Kg ↔ lb equivalents for the gym

Plenty of programs are written in the other unit. This guide helps you translate loads without losing precision.

The conversion

1 kg = 2.2046 lb. To go from kilos to pounds multiply by 2.2046; for the other direction multiply by 0.4536.

Common loads

  • 60 kg ≈ 132 lb
  • 100 kg ≈ 220 lb
  • 140 kg ≈ 309 lb
  • 135 lb ≈ 61 kg
  • 225 lb ≈ 102 kg
  • 315 lb ≈ 143 kg

Remember: kg plates and lb plates don't line up, so a loaded bar is rarely a round number in both units. Use the converter for the exact value.

FAQ

How many pounds is 100 kg?

About 220.5 lb.

How many kilos is 225 lb?

About 102 kg.
